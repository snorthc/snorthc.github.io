Getting started

Install Astrala on your Mac
  1. Download the .dmg from the download page, never from anywhere else.
  2. Check its SHA-256 fingerprint against the release (the download page shows you how).
  3. Open the .dmg and drag Astrala Wallet into Applications.
  4. Open it. If macOS asks whether you're sure, choose Open.
Astrala only ever comes from its official download page. Anyone sending you a "special version" in a DM is trying to steal from you.
Create a wallet: name and password

Choose Create a new wallet, give it a name (it's only a label, shown on your portfolio, and you can rename it any time), pick the currency you think in, then set a password of at least 10 characters.

The password encrypts your recovery phrase on this Mac with Argon2id and AES-256. There is no "forgot password": nobody, including us, has a copy to reset. If you forget it, you restore from your recovery phrase.

Back up your 24-word recovery phrase

Astrala shows you 24 words, then asks you to confirm a few of them. Those words are your wallet: anyone who has them can take everything, on any device.

  • Write them on paper, in order. Keep the paper somewhere safe and offline.
  • Never screenshot them, type them into a website, or store them in cloud notes or a password manager you sync.
  • Astrala blocks screenshots of sensitive screens, but the habit is yours.
No one from Astrala will ever ask for your recovery phrase. Not support, not "verification", not ever.
Receive your first payment

Open Receive, choose the coin and network, and share the address or QR code. Always match the network: sending USDT on Tron to an Ethereum address will not arrive.

The moment the payment is broadcast, Astrala shows it in flight on your Activity screen with an estimate of when it lands, then marks it received once it confirms. Start with a small amount the first time.

Tor, Mullvad & proxy

Route everything through Tor

Open Privacy & route (or Settings → Astral Route) and choose Tor. The first time, Astrala downloads the official Tor binary from the Tor Project and checks its SHA-256 fingerprint; if it doesn't match, it's thrown away.

From then on every request (balances, prices, swap quotes and broadcasts) goes through three Tor relays. The nodes you talk to see a Tor exit relay, never your IP address. Tor is slower than a direct connection; that's the price of the privacy.

Use your Mullvad tunnel

If you use Mullvad VPN, install and sign in to the Mullvad app as usual. In Astral Route choose Mullvad: Astrala connects through it, lets you change location, and has Verify with am.i.mullvad.net so you can confirm traffic really leaves through Mullvad.

Prefer something else? Choose Proxy and enter any SOCKS5 proxy, such as your own server.

How the kill switch protects you

With the kill switch on (it is by default), if Tor, Mullvad or your proxy drops, Astrala holds every request until the route is back. Nothing falls back to your real connection in the gap, not even a balance check.

Security & opsec

Payment PIN and daily spending limit

Set a payment PIN in Settings → Security. Every send and swap then needs it, bound to that exact payment. Too many wrong attempts and Astrala blocks payments for a while.

A daily spending limit caps how much can leave the wallet in any rolling 24 hours. Changing it needs your PIN, so a stranger at your unlocked Mac can't raise it.

Set up a duress PIN

A duress PIN is for the moment someone forces you to open your wallet. On the lock screen you type the duress PIN instead of your password: Astrala opens an empty decoy wallet, and every coin quietly sweeps to a Vault phrase that only you have, on paper.

  1. Settings → Security → Duress PIN → Set up.
  2. Write down the Vault phrase Astrala gives you and store it apart from your main phrase.
  3. Choose a PIN you'll remember under stress.
Sweeping is a real on-chain transfer and pays normal network fees. Keep the Vault phrase safe: it's where your coins go.
Stealth mode, blur when unfocused, screen protection
  • Stealth mode: press ⌘ ⇧ H anywhere to blur every balance and amount.
  • Blur when unfocused: balances blur the moment you switch to another app.
  • Screen protection: Astrala shows up black in screenshots, recordings and screen sharing. Turn it off when you want to share your screen.
  • Clear copied addresses: wipes the clipboard a set time after you copy an address.
Address-poisoning warnings

Attackers create an address that starts and ends like one you use, then send you a tiny payment so it sits in your history, hoping you'll copy it next time. Astrala compares every address you paste against your contacts and history and warns you when one only looks familiar.

Save the addresses you pay often in the Address book; they then show by name when you send.

Swaps & coins

Swap one coin for another

Open Swap, pick what you pay and what you receive, and enter an amount. Astrala asks every decentralized route at once (Jupiter, KyberSwap, ParaSwap, THORChain, Maya, LI.FI and NEAR Intents) and shows the cheapest first, with the rate, the minimum you'll get, the network fee and the arrival time.

Nothing moves until you review it and enter your PIN. The swap is signed on your Mac and goes straight to the protocol: no exchange account, no KYC, no custody.

When swapping a token on Ethereum or similar chains, Astrala approves only the exact amount of the swap, never unlimited, and resets any old approval first.
Add any coin or token

Choose Add a coin, search by name or paste a contract address. Verified tokens are marked; anything else shows a warning so you know to double-check the contract. Added tokens join your solar system like any other planet.

Monero

How Monero works in Astrala

Your Monero keys never leave your Mac. Astrala downloads blocks from a node and scans them locally; the node only serves blocks and never learns which outputs are yours. The first sync takes a while; after that it keeps up in the background.

Use your own Monero node, or rescan

Settings → Monero → Your own node: enter your node's address (for example one running at home) for maximum privacy, or leave it empty to rotate between public nodes.

If a balance ever looks wrong, Rescan clears the local Monero cache and scans again from when the wallet was created.

Backup & recovery

Restore from your recovery phrase

On a new Mac, install Astrala and choose Restore with a recovery phrase. Enter your 24 words and, if you know it, roughly when the wallet was created (it speeds up the Monero scan). Your coins live on the blockchain, not on your Mac, so they all come back.

Encrypted file backups and shares

Settings → Security → Backups offers two extra safety nets:

  • File: one encrypted file with everything (phrase, names, pictures, contacts, alerts). It needs a password to open.
  • Shares: your phrase split into 3 shares, where any 2 restore it. Keep them in different places, and no single share reveals anything on its own.
I forgot my password

There's no reset: your password protects the phrase on this Mac, and no copy exists anywhere else. Restore the wallet from your 24-word phrase (or your backup file or shares) and set a new password.
