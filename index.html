The private crypto wallet for Mac.

Astrala keeps your keys on your Mac and can send everything it does through Tor, Mullvad or your own proxy. There is no account to make and no company holding your coins.

Hide your IP from every service the wallet talks to

Blockchain nodes, price feeds and swap routers normally see your IP next to your addresses. Turn on Tor (built in), your Mullvad tunnel or a proxy, and they see that instead. If the route drops, the kill switch holds requests until it's back.

Stealth mode blurs every amount and address with one shortcut. A duress PIN opens a decoy wallet.

Swap without an exchange account

Each swap is quoted by Jupiter, KyberSwap, ParaSwap, THORChain, Maya, LI.FI and NEAR Intents at the same time. You pick a route and it is signed on your Mac.

You can also chain swaps through one-time wallets, or split one coin into several, each going to a new account.

Incoming payments show up before they confirm

For Bitcoin, Litecoin, Solana and Monero, money sent to you appears as soon as it's broadcast, with an estimate of when it will land.

Sending always goes through a review screen, your PIN, and a system confirmation the app window can't change.
